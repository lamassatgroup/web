FAQ

Got questions?

A concise starting point. We can expand this page later with detailed technical and commercial answers.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timing depends on scope, integrations, product complexity and validation requirements. The first step is a discovery conversation that defines the work.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes. The service structure is suitable for a focused integration, an MVP, an internal system or a larger production platform.

What does the process look like after we agree to work together?

We move through discovery, planning and design, development, testing, launch, then ongoing support and growth.

Who owns the code when the project is done?

Ownership terms should be defined in the project agreement and can be structured around the client's business requirements.

Can you maintain and scale the system after launch?

Yes. Support, updates, optimisation and growth are part of the six-step service model.

What if an AI agent doesn't perform as expected?

Agents should be tested in controlled environments with accuracy checks, guardrails, monitoring and human oversight before production deployment.
